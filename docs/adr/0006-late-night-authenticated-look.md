# Authenticated look is late-night dark; Landing is not the design surface

The toolbox is a late-night listening room: forced dark, near-black canvas, quiet chrome. Green is only Sign-in, Start, Listen on Spotify, and the Library crawl fill — not a theme color and not Spotify’s app chrome. A light theme would be a second product; a clone would fight the hub-and-Tool shape.

This overhaul designs the authenticated surfaces. The Landing stays a Sign-in door in the same canvas (one green button, error under it) so Sign-in does not flash white. The public page itself stays the Landing ticket. Leaving the Landing light, or building that page here, were the rejected splits.

**Considered options:** Dark nod on our layouts, thin dark Sign-in (chosen). Light with green accents. Near-clone of Spotify. Look scoped to `/app` only, Landing left light.

The plain-shell clause in ADR 0002 is superseded by this decision. The Landing/Dashboard split in that ADR still holds.
