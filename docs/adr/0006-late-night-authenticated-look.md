# Authenticated look is late-night dark; Landing is not the design surface

The toolbox is a late-night listening room: forced dark, near-black canvas, quiet chrome. Primary buttons are the CTA green — that is the main verb, not a reserved accent and not Spotify’s app chrome. Secondary actions (Re-shuffle, Install, Sign-out) use quiet variants. A light theme would be a second product; a clone would fight the hub-and-Tool shape.

This overhaul designs the authenticated surfaces. The Landing stays a Sign-in door in the same canvas (one primary Sign-in, error under it) so Sign-in does not flash white. The public page itself stays the Landing ticket. Leaving the Landing light, or building that page here, were the rejected splits. Reserving green for only Sign-in, Start, Listen, and the Library crawl fill was rejected: every primary button should be green.

**Considered options:** Dark nod on our layouts, thin dark Sign-in (chosen). Light with green accents. Near-clone of Spotify. Look scoped to `/app` only, Landing left light. Green only on four named sites.
